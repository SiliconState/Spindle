Privacy policy · updated 31 October 2026

Your work stays yours.

Spindle for Browser is open-source software (MIT) for Google Chrome and the browsers that install from its Web Store (Microsoft Edge, Brave and others). It has no account and no server of ours. What it handles stays in your browser, or goes where you send it. We receive nothing.

What it handles, and where it goes

  • Your threads (your messages, the model's replies, the files you attach and the files a thread makes) are kept in the extension's own storage on this device. What a thread sends goes to the model provider you chose for it, when you send.
  • Pages, only once you turn on page reading (Settings, Pages; off when installed): the text, parts and HTML of the tabs a thread reads go to that thread's model provider. With page actions also on, a thread clicks, types and navigates in your tabs as you, and can record a tab's network requests when a task asks for them; what it records goes to the provider too, once a thread reads it. Screenshots are kept in the thread's workspace.
  • Your keys and sign-ins are encrypted (AES-GCM) in your browser's storage, with an optional passphrase, and sent only to the provider they were saved for (and to OpenAI's sign-in service, if you sign in with ChatGPT). A site's secret header that you save is encrypted the same way and sent only to that site.
  • Commands' network, only once you turn it on for a thread: its commands reach the websites they ask for, through your browser (without your cookies) or through a relay you set up. With a relay, names are looked up over DNS-over-HTTPS (Cloudflare), only while such a command runs.
  • Sites scripts may call: requests go only to the sites you list.
  • Your Spindle host, if you connect one: the commands of threads you run there, with their workspace files, go to that host, which is yours.

What we receive

Nothing. Spindle has no analytics, telemetry, advertising or tracking, and sends us no data. We never sell, transfer or use your data for anything else, and no person at SiliconState reads it.

Who else processes it

The model providers you choose (for example OpenAI, Anthropic, DeepSeek, Z.ai, Moonshot, or a model server you run) process what your threads send them under their own privacy policies. Websites your threads visit see those visits, as they would yours.

How long it is kept

On your device, until you delete the thread or remove the extension (which deletes everything it keeps). A key or sign-in stays until you remove it in Settings, and a site's secret header until you remove the site; if you forget your passphrase, Forgot it deletes them all. Your threads are protected by your browser profile; only keys and secrets are encrypted inside it.

Limited Use

Spindle's use of the information it handles complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements: it is used only to do what you ask a thread to do.

Children

Spindle is not directed at children under 13.

Changes and questions

Changes to this policy are published on this page, with its date. Ask about it, or about anything Spindle does, on GitHub; the source code shows exactly what it does.
